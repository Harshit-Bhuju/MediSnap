# MediSnap - AI-Powered Healthcare Ecosystem

MediSnap is a next-generation healthcare management platform that transforms complex medical reports into clear, actionable health insights. By combining Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, OCR technology, and telemedicine capabilities, MediSnap empowers individuals and families to better understand, monitor, and manage their health.

## 🚀 Vision

Healthcare information should be accessible, understandable, and actionable for everyone. MediSnap bridges the gap between medical data and patient understanding by providing intelligent report analysis, personalized health guidance, and seamless access to healthcare services.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🧠 AI-Powered Medical Report Analysis

* OCR-based extraction from medical reports and diagnostic documents.
* AI-generated summaries that convert complex clinical terminology into easy-to-understand explanations.
* Intelligent insights for blood tests, radiology reports, and health assessments.
* Personalized recommendations based on report findings.

### 📊 Smart Health Monitoring

* Health risk assessment powered by AI-driven analytics.
* Personalized health goals, medication reminders, and wellness tracking.
* Interactive dashboards and visualizations for monitoring health trends over time.
* Historical record comparison for long-term health management.

### 👨‍⚕️ Telemedicine & Consultation Platform

* Online appointment scheduling with healthcare professionals.
* Virtual consultation support and communication tools.
* Dedicated dashboards for patients, doctors, and administrators.
* Centralized patient record management.

### 🎯 Gamified Wellness Experience

* Health-focused challenges and wellness quests.
* Reward system with badges, achievements, and progress tracking.
* AI-powered exercise and posture monitoring using computer vision.
* Daily engagement features designed to encourage healthier habits.

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Health Management

* Manage multiple family member profiles from a single account.
* Centralized medical records and health history.
* Track health trends and reports across the household.
* Simplified caregiving and health monitoring for families.

MediSnap is built with a security-first approach:

* JWT-based authentication
* Protected medical record access
* Secure report uploads and storage
* Environment-based configuration management
* Role-based access control for users, doctors, and administrators

To make healthcare information understandable, accessible, and actionable by leveraging Artificial Intelligence, empowering users to make informed health decisions and proactively manage their well-being.
